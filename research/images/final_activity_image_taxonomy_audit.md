## 3. Reference Visual Research & Candidate Category Evaluation

Using the expanded visual reference research set documented in [`research/images/expanded_activity_visual_references.md`](file:///c:/VScode/GlobeTrotter_Hackathon/research/images/expanded_activity_visual_references.md), we evaluated candidate additions against the 1,734 activity dataset under the **smallest practical taxonomy** rule:

### A. Modern Shopping Malls vs Markets/Bazaars
* **Activities Count in Dataset**: **0 modern indoor multi-story shopping malls**. In our 1,734 dataset, the word "Mall" refers exclusively to open-air hill station promenades (`Shimla Mall Road`, `Mussoorie Mall Road`, `Kasauli Mall Road`).
* **Visual Evaluation**: Open-air hill station Mall Roads are pedestrian shopping streets with heritage buildings. They are visually and semantically covered by `VIBRANT_STREET_MARKET` and `COLONIAL_ARCH`.
* **Taxonomy Decision**: **REJECTED**. Do not create a `MODERN_SHOPPING_MALL` concept (0 matching activities in dataset).

### B. Traditional & Highway Dhabas vs Street Food / Thalis
* **Activities Count in Dataset**: **1 dhaba activity** (`#3256 Kesar Da Dhaba` in Amritsar).
* **Visual Evaluation**: `Kesar Da Dhaba` serves traditional thalis, dal makhani, and parathas in a dining hall setting. It is seamlessly covered by `TRADITIONAL_FOOD_THALI` and `STREET_FOOD_SCENE`.
* **Taxonomy Decision**: **REJECTED**. Do not create a `HIGHWAY_DHABA` concept for 1 activity. `TRADITIONAL_FOOD_THALI` and `STREET_FOOD_SCENE` cover it perfectly.

### C. Existing Concepts That Should Remain Merged
* **`HANDICRAFT_TEXTILE_BAZAAR` & `VIBRANT_STREET_MARKET`**: Keep consolidated. Both represent colorful Indian market streets with vendor stalls and shoppers.
* **`MUSEUM_EXTERIOR` & `MUSEUM_INTERIOR`**: Keep unified under `MUSEUM_EXTERIOR` (0 activities assigned to interior). A grand museum facade is universally clearer on travel cards than an interior glass display case.
* **`PALACE_EXTERIOR` & `PALACE_COURTYARD`**: Keep unified under `PALACE_EXTERIOR` (0 activities assigned to courtyard). Ornate palace facades represent royal palace tours seamlessly.
* **`TROPICAL_SANDY_BEACH` & `PALM_LINED_BEACH`**: Keep unified under `TROPICAL_SANDY_BEACH` (only 2 activities assigned to palm-lined).

## 4. Required NEW Visual Concepts

To eliminate major visual distortions across 40+ high-traffic activities, **3 NEW visual concepts** are recommended:

### 1. `SIKH_GURUDWARA` ("Sikh Gurudwara & Golden Temple Complex")
* **Why Existing Concepts Are Insufficient**: Golden Temple Amritsar, Takht Sri Patna Sahib, and Bangla Sahib Delhi are currently mapped to `TEMPLES_RELIGIOUS_NORTH` (Akshardham Hindu Temple). Hindu sandstone shikhara towers visually contradict the iconic white marble architecture, golden domes, and Sarovar water pools of Sikh Gurudwaras.
* **Affected Activities**: 6 major high-profile activities (`Golden Temple & Langar`, `Golden Temple Harmandir Sahib`, `Takht Sri Patna Sahib`, `Manikaran Sahib`, `Bangla Sahib`).
* **Current Problematic Concept**: `TEMPLES_RELIGIOUS_NORTH` (Akshardham Hindu Temple).

### 2. `DAM_RESERVOIR` ("Water Dam & Hydroelectric Reservoir")
* **Why Existing Concepts Are Insufficient**: 26 major dam and reservoir activities across India (Sardar Sarovar Dam, Banasura Sagar Earth Dam, Mattupetty Dam, Hirakud Dam, Tehri Dam, Bhakra Nangal, Aliyar Dam, Srisailam Dam) are currently mapped to `STONE_ARCH_COMPLEX` (Ellora Caves) or `WILDLIFE_LION`. Showing rock-cut cave walls or lion safari jeeps for massive water dam spillways is an obvious visual mismatch.
* **Affected Activities**: 26 activities (`Sardar Sarovar Dam`, `Banasura Sagar Dam`, `Mattupetty Dam`, `Hirakud Dam`, `Srisailam Dam`, etc.).
* **Current Problematic Concept**: `STONE_ARCH_COMPLEX` (Ellora Rock-Cut Cave 16) / `WILDLIFE_LION`.

### 3. `RESTAURANT_FINE_DINING` ("Restaurant & Indoor Dining Ambiance")
* **Why Existing Concepts Are Insufficient**: Heritage restaurants, dining halls, and indoor bistros (Ambrai Udaipur, Paragon Kozhikode, Cafe des Arts Pondicherry, Peshawri, Johnson's Cafe Manali) are currently mapped to `STREET_FOOD_SCENE` (outdoor street hawker stalls) or `PALACE_EXTERIOR`. Outdoor hawker carts do not accurately represent indoor restaurant dining.
* **Affected Activities**: 15+ indoor restaurant, dining hall, and cafe activities.
* **Current Problematic Concept**: `STREET_FOOD_SCENE` (Indore Street Hawker Stalls).
