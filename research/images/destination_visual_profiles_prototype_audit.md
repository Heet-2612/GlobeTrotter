### 3. Poovar (Catalog #155)
* **Sources Consulted:** [Kerala Tourism](https://www.keralatourism.org/), Kerala Backwater Tourism Board.
* **Hero Subjects Rationale:** Poovar's defining natural phenomenon is its narrow golden sand barrier spit that separates the tranquil backwater mangrove estuary from the open Arabian Sea.
* **Discovered Aliases:** `Poovar Island`, `Poovar Estuary`.
* **Negative Filters:** Commercial harbor cargo ships, city street scenes, generic palm tree close-ups.
* **Visual Guidance Note:** Elevated or wide vistas showing the sand spit separating calm backwater from ocean waves are ideal.
